## 📋 Quick Summary

### In English

MbewuSmart helps Malawian farmers identify crop diseases using AI. We respect your privacy and have built strong protections into the app:

- Your **PIN** is hashed with bcrypt — nobody (including us) can read it.
- Your **National ID** is encrypted with AES-256 and never leaves your phone.
- Your **name and phone number**, when shared with extension officers, are encrypted before being sent to our database.
- **Public reports** (Disease Watch) contain only crop type, disease, and approximate district — no personal information.
- We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** show ads.
- You can **delete all your data** by uninstalling the app.

## 2. What data we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide when registering

When you create an account, you provide:

| Data | How we store it | Where it lives |
|------|------------------|----------------|
| **Full name** | AES-256 encrypted before Firestore upload | Encrypted on your phone (Hive); encrypted in Firestore |
| **Phone number** | AES-256 encrypted before Firestore upload | Encrypted on your phone; encrypted in Firestore |
| **National ID** *(optional)* | AES-256 encrypted with a key in Android Keystore | Only on your phone — **never** sent to cloud |
| **PIN (4 digits)** | bcrypt one-way hash (cost factor 12) | Only on your phone — **never** sent to cloud |
| **District** | Stored as text | On your phone and in Firestore (used for Disease Watch grouping) |

### 2.2 Information generated by using the app

| Data | How we store it | Why we keep it |
|------|------------------|----------------|
| **Crop photos** | Sent to Google Gemini AI for analysis | To identify diseases |
| **AI diagnosis results** | Stored in your scan history | So you can review past scans |
| **Approximate location** | Rounded to ~11km precision (1 decimal place) | To group reports by region in Disease Watch |
| **Anonymous device ID** | Random ID generated on first install | To link your reports without using your name |
| **Scan timestamps** | Stored on your phone and in Firestore | To track disease trends over time |

### 2.3 Information we explicitly **do not** collect

- ❌ Your exact GPS coordinates (we round them)
- ❌ Your contacts, SMS, calendar, or browsing history
- ❌ Photos other than crops you actively scan
- ❌ Camera/microphone access outside of scanning
- ❌ Identifying information you didn't explicitly provide

## 3. How we protect your data

MbewuSmart implements seven layers of security:

1. **bcrypt PIN hashing** with cost factor 12 — prevents brute-force PIN attacks
2. **AES-256-GCM encryption** for National IDs — industry-standard encryption used by governments
3. **Android Keystore** for encryption keys — hardware-backed secure storage on your phone
4. **Encrypted local database (Hive)** — your entire local database is an encrypted blob
5. **Anonymous device IDs in cloud** — no real names link your reports to you
6. **Hybrid data model** — public reports (Disease Watch) contain zero personally identifiable information
7. **Strict Firestore security rules** — server-side validation prevents data tampering

If your phone is stolen or lost, your encrypted data remains protected. Only someone with both your unlocked phone AND your PIN can access your account.

## 4. Third parties we use

To make MbewuSmart work, we share some data with these services:

### 4.1 Google Firebase (Cloud database)
- **What we send:** Encrypted reports, anonymous disease data, anonymous IDs
- **Why:** To enable extension officers and agriculture managers to track disease outbreaks
- **Their privacy policy:** https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### 4.2 Google Gemini AI (Image analysis)
- **What we send:** The crop photo and a text prompt asking for diagnosis
- **Why:** To identify diseases and recommend treatments
- **Their privacy policy:** https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
- **Note:** Photos are sent to Gemini only during active scanning. We do not store them on Google's servers permanently.

### 4.3 OpenStreetMap (Map tiles)
- **What we send:** Anonymous map tile requests
- **Why:** To display the Malawi map on the analytics dashboard
- **Their privacy policy:** https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

We do **not** share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties.

## 5. Disease Watch — public anonymous feed

MbewuSmart includes a **Disease Watch** feature that shows farmers what diseases are being reported across Malawi. This feed is **public** and **anonymous**:

- ✅ Shows: crop type, disease name, approximate district, timestamp
- ❌ Does NOT show: farmer name, phone number, exact location, photo

You can opt out of having your reports appear in Disease Watch by toggling the privacy setting when saving a scan.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:

### 6.1 Delete all your data
**Uninstalling the app deletes all your local data.** This includes your account, PIN, National ID, scan history, and personal information.

To delete data already shared with managers/officers, email us at **zaichikuse@gmail.com**. We will delete it within 30 days.

### 6.2 Know what data we have
Email us at **zaichikuse@gmail.com** and we will send you all data associated with your anonymous ID within 30 days.

### 6.3 Correct your information
Update your name, phone, or district in the app's Settings page at any time.

### 6.4 Opt out of Disease Watch
When saving a scan, toggle "Share with Disease Watch" to OFF. Past reports can be made private by editing them in your scan history.

### 6.5 Withdraw consent
You can stop using MbewuSmart at any time. Uninstalling removes all local data.

## 11. Data transfers outside Malawi

MbewuSmart uses Google Firebase and Google Gemini, which process data in Google's global infrastructure (primarily United States and Europe). This means your encrypted reports may be stored on servers outside Malawi.

We chose Google because:
- They comply with international data protection standards
- Data in transit and at rest is encrypted
- They are widely audited for security

By using MbewuSmart, you consent to this cross-border data processing.
